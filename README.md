## 🔍 Connection with Advanced Digital Forensics

As part of my internship, I explored how ELV (Extra Low Voltage) systems can act as sources of digital evidence. My work in CCTV, access control, networking, IP-PBX systems, procurement and technical documentation helped me understand their relevance to different areas of digital forensics.

### 1. Digital Evidence Acquisition
- Studied CCTV and access control BOQs, including NVRs, hard disks and ACS servers.
- Understood how CCTV footage can be exported or acquired while preserving the original evidence.
- Explored forensic imaging, write blockers and MD5/SHA-256 hash verification using FTK Imager as part of self-learning.

### 2. File System and Data Recovery
- Performed CCTV storage sizing based on camera count, bitrate and recording duration.
- Studied NVR circular overwriting and video retention.
- Explored file carving, unallocated space and recovery concepts.

### 3. Digital Forensic Investigation Tools
Explored the applications of:
- **FTK Imager:** Forensic imaging and hash verification.
- **Autopsy:** File system analysis, timelines and file carving.
- **Wireshark:** Network traffic analysis, including IP-PBX and camera traffic.
- **ExifTool:** Metadata examination.
- **Hashing Utilities:** File integrity verification.

### 4. Network and Email Forensics
- Worked with networking requirements involving fibre, switches, firewalls and Wi-Fi.
- Studied IP-PBX communication and network infrastructure.
- Understood DHCP, firewall and switch logs as potential sources of evidence.
- Explored email header analysis, SPF, DKIM and DMARC to understand spoofed vendor emails and procurement fraud.

### 5. Mobile and Cloud Forensics
- Studied digital traces generated by CCTV mobile applications and access control systems.
- Explored call records associated with GSM gateways and IP-PBX systems.
- Understood how cloud-shared project documents maintain access logs and version history.

### 6. Evidence Preservation and Reporting
- Maintained procurement records through Purchase Orders, delivery challans, site receipts, Material Supply Breakdowns and logistics records.
- Prepared Minutes of Meetings, dashboards and technical reports.
- Understood how documented handovers, timestamps and approvals relate to forensic chain of custody and evidence reporting.

**Note:** The forensic tools and investigation techniques were explored through academic self-learning and tool-to-use-case mapping. They were not used to investigate actual client systems or access confidential company data.
